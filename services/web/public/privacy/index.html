EspHub Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Max & Zoe, Inc. (Okou, “we” or “us”) operates EspHub. This policy explains how we use personal information when you visit the website, use the device console or API, or connect an MCP client such as ChatGPT or Claude.

Account information and cookies

Clerk handles sign-in, session cookies, and OAuth consent. EspHub uses your account identifier to associate devices with your account and check who can access them. Session cookies let the console recognize your signed-in browser. Clearing or blocking them may require you to sign in again. If you email us, we use your email address and the information you send to respond.

Device data and why we use it

  • Registration and connection. We use the device name, model, identifiers, and authentication records to register boards, authenticate connections, and route commands to the intended device.
  • Hardware and software reports. Physical boards can report firmware versions, connection status, boot identifiers, and device events. For cloud-installed Python programs, the board reports the transfer ID, byte count, and hash of the installed file. We make these records available for device management and troubleshooting.
  • Network configuration. A Wi-Fi operation can involve nearby or saved network names and the password you supply to configure a board. Command arguments, and source code can contain personal information or credentials that you or your software include.
  • Virtual devices. If you create a Virtual ESP32, we store its account association, simulated firmware information, boot IDs, and timestamps so you can return to the same board.

We also use access and diagnostic information to maintain the service, investigate failures, and protect accounts and devices.

Information shared with a connected client

EspHub receives the tool name and arguments your client sends. After checking access, it returns the result to that client. A request to inspect a cloud-installed program can return its installation record. With write permission, a client can also request changes to a device.

The client provider handles the information it receives under its own privacy policy. You can disconnect the client to stop future authorized access. Copies of results it already received are subject to that provider’s retention and deletion controls.

Providers and international processing

Clerk provides identity services. Neon stores account, device, and virtual boot records. Cloudflare hosts EspHub, coordinates connections, and stores device records and staged files. For devices using the MQTT connection, AWS IoT handles device certificates, commands, and device reports. GitHub hosts public firmware releases. When you supply a download URL, the board contacts the host you selected. These providers may process information outside your country.

Retention and deletion

Private programs and firmware files staged through EspHub have a 24-hour expiry. That period applies to the staged copy. Software already installed on hardware stays there until you replace or erase it.

Account records, physical device history, and cloud installation records are separate from staged uploads. We use those records to associate devices with an account, enforce access, and provide diagnostics. Removing a physical registration revokes its device token. Removing a virtual registration deletes that board’s saved boot history. Contact us to request deletion of other associated records.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose which OAuth permissions to grant, disconnect a client, and remove registered devices. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, object to or restrict processing, or complain to a data protection authority.

To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, email ethan@okou.ai with your account email and a description of your request. We may ask you to verify ownership. Please leave passwords, tokens, and private serial logs out of your message.

Children’s privacy

EspHub is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address above so we can investigate and address the request.

Policy updates

We will publish updates on this page and revise the effective date. Our Terms of Service explain the rules for using EspHub and managing devices.
